TransparencyA corporate company must provide timely, accurate, and clear disclosures regarding its financial performance, ownership, and governance. Shareholders and stakeholders have a right to know how the company is being managed, and hidden operations or deceptive bookkeeping violate this fundamental pillar

AccountabilityThere must be a clear structure where everyone is answerable for their actions. The management is accountable to the Board of Directors, and the Board of Directors is ultimately accountable to the shareholders. This rule ensures that individuals are held responsible for the company's successes and failures

 ResponsibilityThe corporation must act as a responsible citizen. This means adhering to national and local laws, ensuring a safe workplace environment for employees, and minimizing any negative footprint on society and the environment. The board must also proactively manage and mitigate risks to protect the company's assets

  FairnessA corporation must treat all its stakeholders with equity and respect. This includes protecting minority shareholders' rights alongside majority investors, maintaining fair and non-discriminatory hiring and employment practices, and fostering an inclusive corporate culture

  IndependenceTo avoid conflicts of interest, corporate boards must include independent directors who do not have material or financial ties to the company's daily management. Independent oversight ensures that decisions are made to benefit the company as a whole, rather than serving the personal interests of top executives or specific shareholders.
